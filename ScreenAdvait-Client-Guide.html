📌 Overview

What is ScreenAdvait?

A simple tool that automatically takes screenshots of your employees' computers and stores them securely for you to review anytime.

🖥️

Desktop App

A small app installed on each employee's computer. It runs silently in the background — employees won't see it.

📸

Auto Screenshots

The app automatically captures the employee's screen at intervals you choose (every 5, 15, or 30 minutes).

🌐

Web Portal

View all captured screenshots from anywhere using your secure web dashboard. No software needed — just a browser.

🔄 How It Works

Simple 4-Step Process

Getting started takes just a few minutes.

1️⃣

Login to Web Portal

Open your browser and go to the ScreenAdvait portal. Login with your company admin credentials.

2️⃣

Add Employees

Create employees in the portal. Each employee gets a unique username, password, and license key.

3️⃣

Install Desktop App

Download the app from the portal and install it on each employee's computer. Login once with their credentials.

4️⃣

Monitor & Review

Screenshots start appearing on your portal automatically. Review them anytime, from anywhere.

Step 1

Login to Your Web Portal

Access your company's monitoring dashboard from any browser.

  • 1
    Open the Portal Link Open your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox) and go to:
https://screen.advaitdigital.co.in
  • 2
    Enter Your Login Credentials Type in your Company Admin Username and Password (provided by ScreenAdvait team).
  • 3
    Click "Sign In" You will see your company dashboard with all monitoring features.
Step 2

Create Employees

Add your team members who will be monitored.

  • 1
    Go to "Employees & Keys" tab Click the "Employees & Keys" tab on the left sidebar of your dashboard.
  • 2
    Click "+ New Employee" Fill in the employee's Full Name, Email, Username, and set a Password (minimum 6 characters).
  • 3
    Click "Create" The employee will be added to your list. Repeat for each team member.
💡
Tip: Keep the username and password handy — you'll need them to login on the employee's computer.
Step 3

Generate License Keys

Each employee needs a unique license key to activate the desktop app.

  • 1
    Find the Employee in the Table In the "Employees & Keys" tab, locate the employee you just created.
  • 2
    Click "Generate Key" A unique license key (like ATS-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX) will be created.
  • 3
    Copy the License Key Click on the key to copy it. You'll need this key when setting up the desktop app on the employee's computer.
⚠️
Important: Each license key works on only 1 device. You need a separate key for each employee.
Step 4

Download the Desktop App

Download the installer from your web portal.

  • 1
    Click "Download Desktop App" On the login page of the portal, you'll see a download button. Click it to download the installer file.
  • 2
    Save the File The file ScreenAdvait-Enterprise-Desktop-Setup.exe will be saved to your Downloads folder.
🛡️
Windows Defender / Antivirus Warning: Your browser or Windows may show a security warning. This is normal for new enterprise software. See the next slides for how to allow the app safely.
⚠️ Security — Chrome

If Chrome Blocks the Download

Chrome may show "Virus detected" or "Failed — Blocked" message. This is a false alarm.

  • 1
    Open Chrome Downloads Page Press Ctrl + J or type chrome://downloads in the address bar.
  • 2
    Find the Blocked File Look for "ScreenAdvait-Enterprise-Desktop-Setup" with a "Blocked" or "Virus detected" label.
  • 3
    Click "Keep" or "Keep dangerous file" Click the option to keep the file. If prompted again, confirm by clicking "Keep anyway".
ℹ️
Why does this happen? The app captures screenshots (which looks similar to monitoring software). Chrome is cautious with new apps it hasn't seen before. The app is 100% safe.
🛡️ Security — Windows Defender

If Windows Blocks the Installer

You may see: "Operation did not complete because the file contains a virus." This is a false alarm.

  • 1
    Open Windows Security Press the Windows key, type "Windows Security", and open it.
  • 2
    Go to Protection History Click "Virus & threat protection" → then click "Protection history".
  • 3
    Find & Allow the File Find the entry for "ScreenAdvait-Enterprise-Desktop-Setup.exe". Click on it → click "Actions" → select "Allow on device".
  • 4
    Run the Installer Again Double-click the installer file again. It will now install successfully without any warnings.
🛡️ Alternative Method

Add Exclusion Before Installing

If the above steps don't work, you can add an exclusion first.

  • 1
    Open Windows Security Press the Windows key → type "Windows Security" → open it.
  • 2
    Go to Exclusions Click "Virus & threat protection" → scroll down → click "Manage settings" → scroll to "Exclusions" → click "Add or remove exclusions".
  • 3
    Add the Installer File Click "+ Add an exclusion" → choose "File" → find and select the ScreenAdvait-Enterprise-Desktop-Setup.exe file.
  • 4
    Run the Installer Now double-click the installer. It will run without being blocked.
✅
This is a one-time step. Once the app is installed, it runs without any further issues.
Step 5

Install & Login on Employee's PC

Setup takes less than 2 minutes per computer.

  • 1
    Run the Installer Double-click ScreenAdvait-Enterprise-Desktop-Setup.exe. Follow the installation steps (Next → Install → Finish).
  • 2
    App Opens Automatically After installation, the ScreenAdvait app will open showing a login screen.
  • 3
    Enter Employee Credentials Type in the employee's Username, Password, and License Key that you created in the portal.
  • 4
    Click "Login" The app will connect to the server and start capturing screenshots automatically.

✖ No app window visible

Secret Shortcut (Admin Only): If you ever need to open the app window on any employee's computer, press:
Ctrl + Shift + Alt + S

Step 6

View Screenshots on Your Portal

All captured screenshots appear on your web dashboard in real-time.

  • 1
    Login to Your Portal Go to screen.advaitdigital.co.in and sign in with your admin credentials.
  • 2
    Go to "Desktop Captures" Tab This shows the latest screenshots from all employees, organized by date and employee name.
  • 3
    Click on Any Screenshot Click to view full-size. You can see exactly what was on the employee's screen at that time.
  • 4
    Use "All Screenshots" Tab for Search For advanced browsing with custom pagination and date-wise search.
Step 7

Manage Capture Settings

Control how often screenshots are taken and pause/resume monitoring.

  • 1
    Go to "Capture Settings" Tab In the portal sidebar, click "Capture Settings".
  • 2
    Choose Capture Interval Select how often screenshots should be taken: Every 5 minutes, Every 15 minutes, or Every 30 minutes.
  • 3
    Pause / Resume Monitoring Use the Pause button to temporarily stop all screenshots. Click Resume to start again. Changes take effect within 10 seconds on all employee computers.
⚡
Instant Sync: Any setting change you make on the portal is applied to all employee PCs within 10 seconds — no restart needed.
📊 Monitoring

Device Status Indicators

Track which employees are actively being monitored.

Status What It Means What You Should Do
Active The employee's computer is online and the app is running. Screenshots are being captured normally. Nothing — everything is working fine. ✅
Disconnected The employee's computer has been offline for more than 15 minutes. The app may be stopped, PC is shut down, or no internet. Check if the employee's PC is turned on and connected to the internet. If the app was deleted, reinstall it.
Never Connected The employee was created in the portal but the desktop app has never been installed or logged in on their computer. Install the desktop app on the employee's computer and login with their credentials.
🚨
If an employee deletes the app: Their status will change to 🔴 Disconnected after 15 minutes. You will see this on your portal and can take action (reinstall the app on their PC).
